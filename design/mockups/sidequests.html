B · Decision tree

Which sidequest are you?

A decision tree trained on n = 1. Answer up to five questions and it sorts you into one of my sidequests. Every leaf opens on its own too, and shows the answers that lead to it.

C · Similarity matrix

Sorted by an eigenvector

Each cell compares two sidequests by what they have in common. In alphabetical order it looks like noise. Sorted by an eigenvector, the groups surface as blocks down the diagonal, which suits a site built on Λ.

Similarity is the cosine between two sidequests’ tags. The eigenvector order splits the list on the Fiedler vector (the eigenvector of the second-smallest eigenvalue of the graph Laplacian), then splits each half the same way.
